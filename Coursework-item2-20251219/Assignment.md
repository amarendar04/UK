**AI Statement**

AI tools, including Generative AI, **should not be used to**:

• generate content to include in your assessment submission

• generate code to include in your assessment submission

AI tools, including Generative AI, **can be used to**:

• develop your understanding of the concepts covered in the module

• practice use of analytics and programming skills

• check/improve the clarity of your language

**Use AI tools critically**. They make mistakes and their language is not precise. For example, accuracy and precision may be used as synonyms, while in machine learning these are metrics with very specific meaning and using them interchangeably is not appropriate.

**Coursework 2**

**Please only use your ID anywhere on the coursework or the cover sheet.**

Accurate recognition of traffic lights is critical for Autonomous Vehicles (AVs) to avoid crashes due to red light running which leads to reducing deaths and injuries of people. Hence, in this project, we are looking for an image classification system to classify two classes of traffic lights red and green with high accuracy. Please write a report in 1500 words explaining different stages of achieving the goal of this project. The report should include the following steps in detail. The dataset has been provided for you on Moodle (50%).

2.1. Prepare the dataset for training (Data augmentation, splitting the dataset in training and testing datasets…).

2.2. Design a 2D convolutional neural network to classify these images.

2.3. Provide the training and validation loss and training and testing accuracy plots and report the accuracy of the model.

2.4. Select five testing images (The images should not be in your training dataset e.g. images downloaded from other sources like internet). Please report the predicted labels for all these selected images.

2.5. Please provide two ways (with evidence) to further improve the accuracy of your classifier.
